How We Can Help You

Expert legal representation across real estate transactions and estate planning matters — handled with precision, care, and decades of experience in Ontario law.

01
Residential Real Estate

Buying or selling a home? We handle all aspects of your residential real estate transaction — purchases, sales, refinancing, title transfers, and new build closings — ensuring a smooth, stress-free process.

02
Commercial Real Estate

Expert legal representation for commercial property transactions, including purchases, sales, leasing arrangements, and commercial mortgage refinancing across the Greater Toronto Area.

03
Wills & Estate Planning

Protect your assets and your family's future. We draft comprehensive wills tailored to your unique circumstances, ensuring your wishes are clearly documented and legally enforceable.

04
Estate Administration

Guiding executors and beneficiaries through the estate administration process — probate applications, asset transfers, creditor claims, and final distribution of estates.

05
Powers of Attorney

Drafting powers of attorney for property and personal care, ensuring trusted individuals can make critical decisions on your behalf when it matters most.

06
Notary & Document Services

Professional notarization and certification of documents for legal, real estate, immigration, and government purposes — handled efficiently and accurately.

About the Firm

Decades of Legal Excellence

Kassabian Law Professional Corporation has been serving clients across Toronto and the GTA for decades. Founded by Garen Kassabian, the firm is now led by L. Daniel Hicks, who has continued the tradition of exceptional legal service the firm is known for.

Dan is known for being fully engaged when it matters most — keeping deals alive, navigating complex transactions, and delivering exceptional customer service that consistently exceeds client expectations.

Whether you are a first-time homebuyer, an experienced investor, or planning your estate, we provide clear, honest legal counsel and the expertise to see your matter through to a successful resolution.
